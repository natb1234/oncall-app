2. What OnCall is — and isn't

OnCall is an online platform. We let Posters advertise small, same-day jobs, let Workers bid on them, and collect and hold payment for jobs.

We are not a party to the agreement between a Poster and a Worker. When a bid is accepted, the contract for the job (the "Job Contract") is made directly between the Poster and the Worker. We do not perform jobs, employ Workers, direct how jobs are done, or supply tools or materials. Workers are independent and are not our employees, agents or partners.

We do not check Workers' identity, skills, qualifications, licences, insurance, references or criminal record, and we don't inspect Posters' premises. Profile information is provided by users themselves. You should make your own judgement about who you deal with.

Traders must comply with the law that applies to them, including consumer protection law towards Posters who are consumers, and tax law. If a Worker is not a trader, consumer protection laws that apply between a trader and a consumer will not apply to the Job Contract between that Worker and a Poster.

5. Posting jobs

Describe the job honestly and completely, including its size, timing, location and any hazards (for example, height, heavy lifting, animals, chemicals, sharp tools). Photos must not show other people's faces or personal documents without their permission.

Jobs not allowed on OnCall:

  • driving people, or carrying goods by vehicle for payment where a licence is needed;
  • adult, sexual or escort services; weapons; drugs; anything involving animals being harmed;

We may remove any job that breaks these terms or the law.

6. Bids and the Job Contract

  • A bid is an offer by a Worker to do the job for the price stated. The Poster accepts a bid by tapping Accept and paying.
  • At that moment a Job Contract is formed between the Poster and the Worker on the terms in the job description, the bid and any agreement in their messages, together with these terms.
  • The Worker decides how to do the job, provides their own tools unless agreed otherwise, and is responsible for doing it with reasonable care and skill and in a safe way.
  • The Poster must give the Worker safe access and tell them about any hazards.

7. Payments, fees and payouts

  • Payment processing. Payments are processed by Stripe Payments Europe, Limited. By paying or receiving money through OnCall you also agree to Stripe's terms. We never see or store full card numbers.
  • We collect payment on the Worker's behalf. Each Worker appoints OnCall as their limited agent to receive payment from the Poster. When the Poster pays OnCall, the Poster's obligation to pay the Worker for that job is fulfilled.
  • The hold. When a bid is accepted, the Poster's card is authorised for the bid price and the money is held. It is taken from the card when the job is approved or auto-released (see section 8). Card authorisations generally last about 7 days; if a job isn't completed within that time, we may need to cancel the hold and ask the Poster to pay again.
  • Our fee. The Poster pays the accepted bid price — there's no extra fee for Posters. We deduct a platform fee from the Worker's payout: 10% of the job price, or 6% if the Worker has OnCall Premium at the moment the bid is accepted. The fee is shown to the Worker before they bid and to both sides when the bid is accepted.
  • Payouts. To be paid, a Worker must set up a payout account with Stripe ("Getting paid" on the Profile page) and pass Stripe's identity checks. If a job is approved before that is done, we hold the Worker's share and send it once the payout account is ready.
  • Prices are in euro. Workers who are registered for VAT are responsible for including VAT in their bids.

8. Completion, approval and disputes

  • When the job is finished, the Worker marks it done in the app with a photo of the finished work. No photo, no payout.
  • The Poster then has 24 hours to either approve (which releases payment) or report a problem. If the Poster does neither, payment is released to the Worker automatically. We send a reminder before that happens.
  • If a problem is reported, the automatic release stops. We look at the job description, messages, photos and anything either side sends us, and decide in good faith whether to release the payment to the Worker, refund the Poster in full or in part, or ask for more information. We aim to decide within 10 working days and will tell both sides why.
  • Our decision is about what happens to the money we hold. It doesn't stop either side from using their legal rights against the other, including going to court or the Small Claims procedure.

9. Cancellations and refunds

  • Nothing is charged before a bid is accepted. If you no longer want a job you posted, or want to withdraw a bid, tell the other person in the app or contact us and we'll remove it.
  • After a bid is accepted, contact the other person through the app first. If the job can't go ahead — for example the Worker doesn't turn up, or both sides agree to cancel — contact us and we'll release the hold or refund the Poster.
  • If you're a consumer, the Job Contract is with the Worker, not with us. Because jobs are booked for a specific date, any statutory right to cancel a distance contract does not apply to the job once it has been fully performed at your request.

10. Keep payments on OnCall

For jobs found on OnCall, don't arrange payment outside the app (cash, bank transfer or another app). If you do, the payment hold, the approval window and our dispute help won't apply to that job, and we may suspend your account.

11. Safety

  • Posters: describe hazards, keep pets and children away from the work area, and make sure the Worker can work safely.
  • Meet sensibly: tell someone where you are going, and keep communication in the app.
  • If anyone is in danger, call 112 or 999 first. Then tell us through the app.
  • We don't supervise jobs and can't guarantee anyone's conduct, the quality of work or the safety of premises.
